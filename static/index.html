Plan a citation check

For every citation, the checker extracts the claim, finds the cited paper, reads the relevant passage and reports whether it supports the claim, with the exact quote. Problems are flagged for a human to review.

arXiv IDs give the most accurate results: the checker reads the paper's LaTeX source and bibliography directly, so every citation maps to the right reference.

How a check runs

Every citation rides this line. Interchanges are where a Nemotron model takes over.

  1. Parsing paperLaTeX source or PDF → citing sentences and the bibliography
  2. Resolving referencesarXiv, OpenAlex, Crossref and Europe PMC; full text wherever it is open
  3. Extracting claims Nemotron NanoWhat each sentence says the cited work contains
  4. Retrieving evidenceThe five closest passages of the cited paper, plus its abstract
  5. Judging claims Nemotron SuperA verdict with a quote verified word for word in the source
  6. Escalating to Ultra Nemotron UltraReads the whole cited paper when a verdict needs a second look
  7. ReportEvery citation underlined by its verdict, with the evidence beside it
Powered by NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory: Nano extracts claims, Super judges them, Ultra reads the full cited paper when a verdict needs a second look.

How to read this report

Each citation in the paper is underlined by what the cited work says about the claim attached to it. Select a citation to see the claim and the evidence side by side.

On the line map, the paper's sections are stations. In the section you are in, each citing sentence is a row (s1, s2, …) and each cited work is a stop with its own verdict. An interchange marks a section where Nemotron Ultra re-read a source.
