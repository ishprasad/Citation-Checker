Citation Checker

Does the cited source actually say what the paper claims it says?

For every citation, the checker extracts the claim, finds the cited paper, reads the relevant passage and reports whether it supports the claim, with the exact quote. Problems are flagged for a human to review.

arXiv IDs give the most accurate results: the checker reads the paper's LaTeX source and bibliography directly, so every citation maps to the right reference.
